**Target.** The same quantity (alcohol, speeding, unbelted, drug deaths and shares) published in reports/policy_analysis.md, road_deaths.md, factor_contributions.md, findings.md, README and the website.

**Reproduction.** Everything reconciles to the committed tables.
- deaths/annual.csv: the 2022-24 mean of alcohol_impaired is 12,581.1, of speeding 11,786.0, of all deaths 41,000.0. These are policy_analysis.md's 12,581 / 11,786 / "41,000".
- deaths/segments.csv: the 2016-24 per-year figures are 11,757.9 (alcohol), 10,965.9 (speeding) and 10,488.9 (unbelted). 105,821/353,706 = 29.9%.
- Scripts: scripts/audit/red_team/G02/01_drug_unknown.py and 02_drug_union.py.

## Verdict
**WEAKENED**, confidence high. There is no genuine numeric contradiction. The 12,581 vs 11,800 and 30.7% vs 29.9% pairs are period differences, and I reproduced both. The weakness is unlabelled or confusing variants. The drug count appears with two definitions about 1.9x apart and no reconciliation (#5, #6). The belt count appears with three definitions (#9). The 2022-24 vs 2016-24 period is not restated at several points of use (#1).

**Most damaging issue.** A reader of policy_analysis (drugs ≈ 5,000 a year, 12%) and factor_contributions/README (drug-positive driver, 84,207 deaths, 23.8%) is not told that these are different variables (crash-level police field vs any-driver `drv_drug_any`). The label "determinate crashes only" is also not what the code does.

## Recommended corrections
- State the period beside every "a year" figure (policy §6; road_deaths §3; website heading and tile).
- Add a one-line variable definition to the drugs rows.
- Replace "determinate crashes only" with "any driver positive; negatives require all drivers tested".
- Footnote 96,706 vs 94,400.
- Change "FARS attributes" to "FARS records ... involving".
- In findings §11 add "(vs 12,581 deaths involving alcohol, policy_analysis)".
