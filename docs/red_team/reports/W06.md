# W06 red-team report: motorcycles and helmets

## 1. Target
Website key finding 5 and #motorcycles; road_deaths.md §7, §9; findings.md §8. Claims: motorcyclist deaths +23% (2019 to 2024); 37.9% of those killed with known helmet use were unhelmeted (40.4% in 2016, 34.0% in 2024); in rider-passenger pairs a helmet was associated with a 31% (20-40%) lower risk of death (double pair comparison, DPC); about 641 a year (417-835) of the 18,789 unhelmeted dead "would have been expected to survive" (model-based).

## 2. Reproduction
- Counts and shares reproduced from raw interim FARS (scripts/audit/red_team/W06/01_reproduce.py): 51,254 killed occupants on motorcycles (A_BODY 7), 49,589 helmet known, 18,789 unhelmeted, 0.3789. 5,044 in 2019 and 6,228 in 2024, +23.47%. Year shares match the table, 0.404 down to 0.340.
- DPC reproduced exactly with the project's occupant frame (03_project_pairs.py): 4,735 pairs, RR 0.6929, reduction 30.7%. 641.08 per year = 18,789 x 0.307 / 9.
- The pair set is small. Of 4,735 pairs only 455 are helmet-discordant (149 driver helmeted, 306 passenger helmeted). The four DPC components rest on 86-124 deaths each.

## 4. Verdict
**WEAKENED**, confidence medium-high. The descriptive claims (+23.5%, 37.9%, state spread) survive. The effectiveness claim and the 641/year figure are directionally robust (a helmet effect shows in every subsample and recoding), but they are overstated in certainty.

Most damaging issue: the DPC helmet estimate has no misreporting sensitivity, unlike belts, and its tipping point is only 5.6% (reproduced). If 1 in 18 helmeted survivors were recorded as helmeted but were not, the 31% reduction vanishes. The helmeted survivors' recording is the weak point (outside knowledge: police and self-reports in survivors are not verified like a helmet on a corpse). This is not an attack that proves misrecording exists; it shows the published CI covers sampling error only. The result also rests on 455 discordant pairs, and 641/year applies to 18,789 deaths of which only 15.7% were on two-up bikes. Note also that stable RR across strata does not test this.

## 5. Recommended correction
- Add helmet misreport scenarios (2%, 5%, 10%) to `matched/double_pair_comparison` and `deaths/potential_lives`, with the tipping point 5.6%.
- State in §7, the site and limitations.md that the helmet figure depends on survivors' recorded helmet use, rests on about 455 discordant pairs, and transports a two-up estimate to all riders.
- Say "motorcyclist" includes mopeds, ATVs and three-wheelers. Reword "less likely to die than an unhelmeted companion" to match the DPC estimand.
- Add that the falling unhelmeted share reflects faster growth in helmeted deaths (unhelmeted deaths rose 9% over 2019-2024).
