**Target.** Website key finding 7 and #trend; road_deaths.md Summary 7 and §9: "The 2020-21 surge was steepest in risky driving and on urban roads. From 2019 to 2021 deaths in crashes involving a driver without a valid licence rose 44%, hit-and-run deaths 43% and drunk-driving deaths 33%, against 19% for all deaths"; "deaths on foot, on bicycles and on motorcycles have stayed high". Tables: deaths/segments, driver_records_by_year, annual.

**Reproduction** (scripts/audit/red_team/W08/01_reproduce_and_coding.py, from raw FARS interim acc_aux, vehicle, person):
all +18.9%, rural +6.5%, urban +29.1%, hit-and-run (A_HR=1) +43.2%, invalid-licence crash +44.0% (6,964 -> 10,025). Alcohol (MI) 10,195.5 -> 13,598.6 (+33.4%) was read from segments.csv (not recomputed). Definitions: deaths (FATALS) in crashes with at least one driver (PER_TYP 1) whose L_STATUS is 0-4. Status 6 is valid; 7/9/other is unknown and counted as not invalid. Urban/rural is A_RU 1/2; code 3 (unknown) is in neither. 2024 vs 2019 (pv +1.5, motorcyclists +23.5, pedestrians +12.9, cyclists +28.4) matches segments.csv. Driver-level invalid share 13.0 / 16.7 / 13.9% matches driver_records_by_year (known-status drivers only).

## Verdict: SURVIVES (confidence: high on the numbers, medium on the "steepest in risky driving" framing)
All headline figures reproduce exactly, and the excess over all deaths is large and robust to definition, overlap, baseline and endpoint. The biggest issue is framing, not arithmetic. "Risky driving" is an editorial bundle: it contains hit-and-run (a post-crash act) and invalid licence (a driver status), while "other vehicle occupants" and "restraint unknown" rose as fast as drunk driving and are not mentioned. The invalid-licence figure depends on counting expired/cancelled statuses (3-4), which rose +77% and which may partly reflect pandemic licence extensions (outside knowledge). Also, the drunk driving, speeding and urban rises are roughly 30%, close to each other, so the stated order is not statistically resolved.

## Recommended correction
1. Say "among the steepest" rather than "steepest", and note that invalid-licence is +40% when expired/cancelled statuses are excluded.
2. Add a limitation line on L_STATUS (unknown 3.0% to 3.9%; expired/cancelled definition).
3. Note the pre-pandemic urban/rural divergence (2016-19).
4. Say that hit-and-run deaths are still +35% above 2019 in 2024, or drop "stayed high" for other groups only.
