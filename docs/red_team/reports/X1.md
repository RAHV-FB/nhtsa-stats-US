## 3 Overall assessment
MINOR ISSUES (confidence medium). The leakage gate is real and well used; the main leaks are disclosed single imputations and the FARS-benchmarked weights. The test years were not used for any fitting choice that I found.

Most damaging: the FARS-benchmarked weights (issue 2). They make the fatal numerator an external FARS quantity and bring FARS information from the test years into weighted evaluation and the F08 RR of 1.25; my proxy suggests the post-2020 RR could be nearer 1.13, but the proxy is crude and I do not claim it is correct.

## 4 Recommended corrections
Register alcohol_im and speeding as "pre_crash (severity-dependent ascertainment)" and say so in the report text. State the benchmarking sensitivity for F08. Add a FARS-MI caveat (injury may be in NHTSA's imputation model) to limitations.md and report a measured-only BAC sensitivity. Finish the ejection test for the belt design.
