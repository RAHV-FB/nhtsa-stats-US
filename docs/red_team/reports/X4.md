## 3 Overall assessment: MINOR ISSUES (confidence medium; large parts untested)
Estimators are numerically stable (ridge, ATT vs ATE, positivity, period interactions all move headline RRs by <=2% or not at all). The most damaging issue is #5/#2: single-model adjustment. Headline RRs for night (1.62), interstate (0.70), speed limit (2.44/2.30) and heavy truck depend on adjustment set and on averaging over crash types. In my alternative sets interstate flips from 0.70 to 1.37 and night rises to 3.65. The report text mostly states the conditional meaning (same speed limit; Ax for night), so this is wording and presentation rather than error, but README/website headline forms read as total associations. No published conclusion reverses under any test I ran, though "interstates reverse" holds only conditional on speed limit and junction.

## 4 Recommended corrections
Add Ax-type total-association columns for interstate, speed limit and alcohol/speeding and quote them beside the adjusted figures in README/website; state that effects are heterogeneous by crash type (Wald p<0.001 for speeding, alcohol, motorcycle, heavy truck) and that standardised RRs are averages; show a calibration plot for models A/B; run the QBA on adjusted RRs; finish seat/age heterogeneity for the lives-saved extrapolation.
