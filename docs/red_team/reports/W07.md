# W07 red-team report: vehicle age

## 1. Target
Website key finding 6 and #vehicles, and road_deaths.md Summary 6 and §8. Occupants of vehicles 18 or more years old had 1.8 to 2.2 times the risk of dying of occupants of vehicles under 4 years old. Model A gives 2.22 (1.99–2.48) and model B gives 1.76 (1.59–1.94). The FARS share of killed passenger-vehicle occupants in 18+ vehicles rose from 18.0% to 30.5%.

## 2. Reproduction
Script 01 refits the project's own `person.fit_model` and `factor_contrasts` on the CRSS LPV occupant population (912,573 occupants, 6,949 deaths, age 8+).
- Model A gives 2.219 (1.986–2.480).
- Model B gives 1.756 (1.589–1.940).
- Both match the published values.

The FARS 18+ shares of 0.1799 (2016) and 0.3048 (2024) are read from `deaths/vehicle_age.csv`, not recomputed.

Definitions:
- Vehicle age is the crash year minus the model year, clipped at 0.
- Unknown model year is its own level (n=1,276), not excluded.
- The outcome is a recorded death.
- The design-based variance uses 42 df.
- Model A includes YEAR, region, urbanicity, speed limit, speeding and alcohol.

## 3. Attacks
Attacks 1–6 are empirical, from `01_reproduce_and_variants.py`. Attacks 7–9 were not tested.

## 4. Verdict: SURVIVES, with minor wording issues. Confidence: medium-high.
The headline numbers reproduce exactly. The direction and size hold, and the RR is above about 1.4 (interval) in all 12 variants run. The result is not fragile to period, population, adjustment set or design variance.

The most damaging unresolved issue is attacks 7 and 8, which I did not test. Under-reporting of minor crashes involving old vehicles would shrink the denominator and inflate the death risk. The "risk of dying in a crash" wording, and the trend in the 18+ share of deaths, cannot be separated from fleet composition with FARS alone. The documented hedge on the share is adequate, but the website's "growing share of the dead" sits next to the "far more likely to die" claim, which invites a causal reading.

## 5. Recommended correction
- State plainly on the website that the RR is conditional on a police-reported crash and is an association. Occupant age, belting and speeding were adjusted, but vehicle safety features and maintenance are not distinguishable from age.
- Say that the 1.8–2.2 range is spread across models A and B, and that single-vehicle crashes give about 1.7.
- Add a CRSS-based exposure comparison for the share trend: the weighted CRSS share of LPV occupants in 18+ vehicles, by year.
