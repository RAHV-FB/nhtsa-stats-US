## 4. Verdict
WEAKENED (high confidence on the numbers, medium on the severity of the wording). The counts, shares and growth rates are exactly reproducible and do not depend on the GVWR coding break. The issue is interpretation. The 85% / 71% is a descriptive share that mostly reflects the presence of lighter vehicles and pedestrians in truck crashes, and it says nothing on fault, risk per truck or per mile. Single-truck and multi-truck crashes have roughly 55% non-driver deaths, against 97-98% in truck-plus-car crashes. The headline pools these.

Most damaging issue: the single 85% figure, placed next to an unqualified "were not the truck driver", invites a causal or fault reading. Note also that the 15.7% / 6.1% growth is of deaths in truck crashes (including non-occupants), not of truck-caused deaths.

## 5. Recommended correction
Add: "These are counts of people killed in crashes that involved a large truck. They do not show who caused the crash. The share outside the truck is high because most of these crashes involve a lighter vehicle." Optionally show the split by crash type (single truck about 55%, truck with a car about 97%) and the comparison with cars, about 50%.
