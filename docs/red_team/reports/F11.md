## 4. Verdict
**WEAKENED (wording only), confidence medium-high.** Every number reproduces exactly, no test-year contamination was found, and the "confirmed" interactions persist. What does not hold is the sentence "Inputs exclude anything recorded after the crash ... enforced by the same leakage gate". The gate only checks a registry label; `speeding` is labelled `pre_crash` while its own note says it is a police judgement recorded after the crash, `alcohol_im` is a post-crash test/imputation, and "unknown" levels act as hit-and-run proxies. These three inputs buy about 0.014-0.016 AUROC and 0.165 -> 0.143 AP (feature_sensitivity.csv; my 01/03 reproduce the AUROC loss). The crash-configuration stage is by construction a description of the crash itself. The caveat appears only in the Limitations paragraph and docs, contradicting the lead statement.

Most damaging issue: the headline sentence on leakage, plus presenting the 0.47 low-risk-half O/P without noting that it rises to 0.59 (HGB) / 0.76 (logit) when the two post-crash judgements are removed.

## 5. Recommended correction
Replace the sentence with: "Inputs exclude outcome fields and consequences of the crash. Police-recorded speeding and imputed alcohol, and the crash configuration, are determined after or at the crash and are included; without speeding and alcohol the boosted AUROC is 0.904 (0.894-0.914)." Report the weighted and unweighted AUROC side by side. State that intervals ignore training/selection variance (already stated), and that the Holm family covers only the 3 screened candidates.
