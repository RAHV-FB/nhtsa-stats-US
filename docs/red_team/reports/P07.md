# P07 red-team report: "No individual attribution"

## 1. Target
"The case explorer shows that 'shares' of a modelled risk change with scale and order and often require combinations that do not exist in the data." Source: reports/case_comparisons.md Summary and sections 1-4; README Phase-2 finding 7; tables outputs/tables/cases/scenarios and scenario_contrasts.
Worked case: exceeded limit + alcohol + unbelted + night + single-vehicle. Quoted numbers: 176 drivers, 52 deaths, observed 19.3%, standardised 15.9% (11.8-21.2%), alcohol-removed RR 1.41 (0.84-2.35) "not distinguishable from no change", single-vehicle comparison "unsupported".

## 2. Reproduction (scripts/audit/red_team/P07/01_reproduce.py)
Reproduced exactly. Standard risk 0.1595 (0.1178-0.2123). RR vs reference 308.4 (209.8-453.3). One-factor-removed RRs: speeding 2.95, alcohol 1.41 (0.84-2.35), unbelted 5.47, night 1.26 (0.70-2.27). Observed rate 0.1925 and in-cell mean prediction 0.1927. The worked case has 44 PSUs in 23 strata, and the largest single weight is 3.1% of the cell total.

## 4. Verdict: SURVIVES, with wording issues (WEAKENED on one sub-claim). Confidence: medium-high.
The headline conclusion (no unique individual attribution) survives. The shares do vary 35-94% in the supported case, and the support limits are real. The sub-claim that alcohol is "not distinguishable from no change" is fragile. The estimate is 1.28-1.92 across specifications, and two of them have CIs excluding 1.

Most damaging issue: the alcohol 1.41 is read as null from one narrow cell with a CI that also includes 2.35. Specification and context change both the point estimate and whether the CI excludes 1.

## 5. Recommended correction
- Replace "not distinguishable from no change" with "imprecise (0.84-2.35); pooled or less saturated specifications give 1.3-1.9".
- Disclose the n>=30 and deaths>=10 rule as arbitrary (the single-vehicle RR would be 1.55, 0.72-3.32).
- Rename the 19.3%/19.3% row as "fit by construction".
- Fix the "96 cases in the main model" text.
