# F05 red-team report: "Some combinations amplify each other"

## 1. Target
reports/findings.md §6 (Finding 5), reports/interactions.md, README finding 5. Pedestrian x unlit darkness "clearest case of amplification on both scales" (IC 93.6/1,000, 70.1-117.1; ratio of RRs 1.62, 1.39-1.88); motorcycle x alcohol additive-super but sub-multiplicative (0.63); speeding x darkness (0.94) and young x night (1.19) "not detected" for fatal; KA speeding x unlit IC 15.5 (8.7-22.2), Holm p 0.0003; speeding x wet "reflects the mix of speeding types ... and the lower wet-road baseline".

## 2. Reproduction
Estimand: one survey-weighted logistic (model A, or A + crash_type/n_vehicles for the pedestrian pair), one product term; p_ab are predictive margins with each factor set to its exposed/reference level-mixture for ALL crashes (pedestrian: reference = the 9 other crash types, so the standardisation population is the whole CRSS crash population, not pedestrian crashes). Tables (pairs_fatal.csv) give p00/p10/p01/p11 = 5.0/84.0/6.6/179.2 per 1,000.
`scripts/audit/red_team/F05/01_ped_unlit_variants.py` V0 reproduces: p = 4.97/84.04/6.56/179.24, IC 93.6 (70.7-116.5, normal CI vs published t CI 70.1-117.1), RRR 1.615 (1.391-1.875). Null pair read from pairs_fatal: speeding x unlit IC 1.8 (-0.7 to 4.3), RRR 0.94 (0.73-1.20), Holm 1.0. Headline numbers reproduce.

## 4. Verdict: SURVIVES for the headline (pedestrian x unlit amplification, motorcycle x alcohol sub-multiplicativity, the fatal nulls); WEAKENED for two secondary statements. Overall: WEAKENED (minor-to-moderate). Confidence: high on the pedestrian pair, medium on the speeding x wet explanation.

Most damaging issue: findings.md §6 asserts that the speeding x wet pattern "reflects the mix of speeding types and the lower wet-road baseline". Data in the project's own tables do not support a full explanation: restricting to limit exceedance leaves the KA deficit intact or larger (RRR 0.78 vs 0.87; IC -26.3 vs -7.1 per 1,000), the fatal additive deficit remains significant, and the evidence rests on an exploratory pair with 67 events in the exposed cell (pairs_fatal exceeded_limit x wet p11_n_events). The longer interactions.md text is properly hedged; the headline is not.

Secondary: the pedestrian IC (93.6) is a property of standardising to all crashes (57.6-94 across populations), so "per 1,000" magnitudes are not interpretable as excess in the actual pedestrian population; the ratio-scale conclusion is stable (1.5-1.9 across variants) and a fully stratified model gives a larger one (2.27).

## 5. Recommended correction
Change findings.md §6 to "partly reflects ... (exploratory; a deficit remains for fatal-or-serious crashes)", matching interactions.md. State that the additive IC magnitude depends on the standardisation population (all crashes), and that the KA additive excess for speeding x unlit is not matched by a Holm-significant ratio-scale result. Optionally add the stratified-model check (script 02) as a functional-form sensitivity.
