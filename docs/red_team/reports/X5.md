## 3 Overall assessment: MINOR ISSUES (confidence: medium-high)
The inference engine is sound, and the report text is more careful than most: absence-of-evidence language appears in findings.md and interactions.md, multiplicity is handled for registered families, and the belt "does not change" claim is genuinely supported by equivalence within about 8%. The weaknesses are of wording and labelling.

Most damaging issue: the speeding-type split (F03). It is the single most publicised finding that contradicts a common belief ("too fast for conditions" has no excess), it is labelled exploratory only in findings.md s14, and it is carried to README, website and policy ledger without that label. The null half rests on 1.06 (0.89-1.26): equivalence is only supported within about +/-22% (MDE80 1.28), the same exposure shows a significant 1.18 (1.03-1.36) on the KA outcome, and "no detectable excess" is then used as "none detected" in policy_analysis for enforcement. The data show "no large excess" not "no excess".

## 4 Recommended corrections
1. README 3, website #speed, policy_analysis: replace "no detectable excess"/"little or no excess" by "no excess larger than about 25% (fatal; 90% CI 0.91-1.22), and a small excess for fatal-or-serious (1.18)". Add "(exploratory; added after the first results)".
2. findings.md s14: move RQ5 and the within-circumstance subgroup table to exploratory, citing change log v1.1; add the speeding-type split to the README/website labelling.
3. Rain: add "in model B, which also fixes crash type, the ratio is 0.90 (0.81-1.00)".
4. Speeding within-circumstance 1.38: state "unadjusted for about 17 subgroups; differs from alcohol (p=0.0001) but not from motorcycle (p=0.24) or clearly from non-speeding, non-alcohol crashes (p=0.010 unadjusted)".
5. README 5: restore "a non-significant result is not evidence of no interaction".
6. Alcohol 5.4 vs 3.8: add the test (ratio of RRs 1.24, 1.03-1.49, p=0.02 in a common-covariate model) or say "not formally tested".
7. 2023-24 definition difference: cite `trends/outcome_definitions_by_year` share ratio 1.09 (1.06-1.12) as the direct test.
8. limitations.md s3: say that only the 13 registered pairs and the multifactor family are multiplicity-adjusted; all other tables (subgroup, period, KA) are unadjusted.
