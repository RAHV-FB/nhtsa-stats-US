# W01 red-team report: "59.6% of deaths involved at least one of four behaviours"

**Verdict: SURVIVES, with minor wording issues (confidence: high on the numbers, medium on the wording judgement).**

## 1. Target
- Website #behaviours heading: "Most deaths involved drink-driving, speeding, or a victim without a belt or helmet". Hero text: "of deaths involved at least one of four behaviours ... a drunk driver, a speeding driver, a victim without a seat belt, or a motorcyclist without a helmet".
- road_deaths.md Summary 1 and §2: 59.6% union; shares 29.9 / 27.9 / 26.7 / 5.3; sum 89.8%; overcount ratio 1.51; "The union is a lower bound in one respect, because belt use is unknown for 5.9% of all deaths."
- Tables: `deaths/segments`, `deaths/behaviour_summary`, `deaths/behaviour_overlap`.

## 2. Reproduction
Script 01 rebuilds the death frame (353,706 deaths, INJ_SEV=4, 2016–2024) from the interim FARS tables using `deaths.build_death_frame`. It then computes the union per implicate and averages over the 10 implicates.

Definitions confirmed:
- **Alcohol:** `midrvacc.A{m}` >= 8, so any driver in the crash at .08+ (crash-level). The 733 deaths with no midrvacc row (0.21%) count as not impaired.
- **Speeding:** `A_SPCRA`==1 (crash-level).
- **Unbelted:** passenger-vehicle occupant (`A_PTYPE` 1–2, `A_BODY` 1–5) with `A_RESTUSE`==2 (victim-level).
- **Unhelmeted:** motorcycle occupant (`A_BODY`==7) with `A_HELMUSE`==2 (victim-level).
- **MI handling:** the union is computed per implicate, then averaged. The code is correct.

## 4. Verdict and most damaging issue
**SURVIVES.** The number is reproduced to five decimals under the stated definitions. The union is stable across implicates and the stated direction of the lower bound is right.

Most damaging issue: the headline mixes crash-level factors (any driver drunk, any vehicle speeding) with victim-level ones (this person was unbelted or unhelmeted). 46% of the "involved" deaths carry only a crash-level factor. They include pedestrians and passengers of sober drivers who were hit by someone else's drunk or speeding driver. Under a stricter "own vehicle" reading the figure is about 50%, so "Most deaths" would be borderline. The published wording ("involved", "not necessarily by the same driver") is defensible, but the hero line "a drunk driver, a speeding driver, a victim without a seat belt" does not say that the driver need not have been in the victim's vehicle. The risk-vehicle definition in my strict variant is the project's own `risk_alc`/`risk_spd`. I did not verify how the project picks that vehicle in multi-vehicle crashes, so 50.0% is an indicative, not definitive, alternative.

## 5. Recommended correction
- Add the size of the lower-bound gap: "if unknown belt/helmet use followed the known rates the union would be about 61%".
- Add one sentence on the unit mix: "46% of these deaths involved only a drunk or speeding driver in the crash, not the victim's own behaviour".
- Do not describe the figure as a lower bound without noting that alcohol is imputed (that part is unbiased in expectation, not a bound).
