# F04 red-team report: "The most robust severity associations involve objective circumstances"

**Verdict: WEAKENED (minor-to-moderate), confidence medium.** Direction and magnitudes of every headline number stand. The wording "as severe as" and "robust" is stronger than the evidence.

## 4. Verdict
WEAKENED, confidence medium. Every attack left the direction and rough size of each headline RR intact; the alcohol IM check found no evidence of leakage. The damage is to wording and nuance.

Most damaging issue: "50-55 mph is as severe as 70+ mph" holds only under the interstate-adjusted model A. The estimate comes from interstate-adjusted comparisons, and 70+ crashes are mostly interstate. Without that adjustment, 70+ is about 24% less severe (0.76, 0.65-0.89). Even within model A the ladder dips at 60-65 (0.76). The "as severe as" claim is also a null-based statement (the data are compatible with 70+ being up to 21% lower).

## 5. Recommended correction
Replace "are as severe as" with "show similar adjusted risk to (RR 70+ vs 50-55 = 0.94, 0.79-1.12 given interstate adjustment; lower without it)". Note the 60-65 dip. Say that alcohol and speeding are police-reported rather than "objective". Add that the pedestrian speed RR depends on the adjustment for location and light (6.6 vs 12.2 without). Report the SUV share among known striking vehicles.
