# P06 red-team report: "No defensible single ranking"

## 1. Target
"Exceeding the limit ranks first on adjusted risk (P ≈ 0.97); alcohol ranks first on deaths involved and attributed burden; predictive importance depends on the measure. The hypothesised ranking drugs > alcohol > belts > speeding is not supported." Config carries 47% of explained log-likelihood, environment 20%, alcohol 8%, speeding type 6%. Sources: reports/factor_contributions.md Summary 3-4, §5; README P2-6; tables contributions/{ranking,rank_probabilities,predictive,predictive_lattice,burden_shapley}.

## 2. Reproduction
- P ≈ 0.97 is a Monte Carlo (20,000 draws) from the JOINT normal approximation of three log-RRs (exceeded, other speeding, alcohol) from ONE design-based logit (env + both behaviours + driver/vehicle mix), joint Taylor covariance. Only sampling error. Script 01: MC P(exceeded rank 1) = 0.9688; RR 5.197 vs 4.112 (matches the table). Pairwise z-test gives 0.9665. Estimates correlation is -0.03, so independence vs joint makes no difference (p_indep 0.9684).
- Config share: lattice Shapley gives 46.6% (script 02), published 47%. Env 20.3%, alcohol 7.5%, speeding 6.0%: matches.

## 4. Verdict: WEAKENED (confidence medium-high)
Direction survives every statistical attack: exceed > alcohol in every adjustment set (ratio 1.26-1.91), the correlation is negligible, config share is stable at about 47%. What is overstated is the certainty and one wording. Most damaging issue: P ≈ 0.97 (and "Stable", "≥ 0.97") is a sampling-only probability for a 1.26x RR gap whose breakeven against differential recording of exceedance is only s = 0.79, in a project whose own limitations say the speeding association is fragile to exactly that. Second: "hypothesised ranking not supported" conflates "drugs cannot be placed" with "ranking refuted"; the alcohol>speeding part is supported, the drugs part untestable, and "alcohol first on deaths involved" would flip if just 9.7% of undetermined-status deaths were drug-positive.

## 5. Recommended correction
- State P ≈ 0.97 as "sampling probability, conditional on police-recorded exceedance being equally complete in fatal and non-fatal crashes; reverses if non-fatal recording is ~21% less complete".
- Replace "not supported" by "cannot be evaluated (drugs unrankable); the alcohol > speeding component holds on deaths and attributed burden, not on per-crash risk".
- Qualify "alcohol first on deaths involved" with "among rankable factors; drug-involved deaths unknown (would exceed alcohol at a 9.7% positive rate among undetermined)".
- Drop or qualify "speeding type loses more when removed last" (flips by test years).
