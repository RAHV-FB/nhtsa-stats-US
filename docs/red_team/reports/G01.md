# G01 red-team report: policy conclusions and break-even arithmetic (reports/policy_analysis.md 3-7)

## 1. Target
"To cut all road deaths by 1%, 5% or 10% through one segment alone, this share of the segment's deaths would have to be prevented" (s4; speeding 3.5%, exceeded limit 8.7%, alcohol 3.3%, drugs 8.2%, unrestrained 4.0%). s6: "Any projection of a camera programme should therefore target limit-exceeding crashes"; "Belt enforcement. This is the closest the data come to an effect estimate"; drug status "unknown for about half of fatal crashes". s7: cost and break-even tools.

## 2. Reproduction
Script 01_segments.py (run, FARS crash file). Per-year deaths and the 2022-24 mean reproduce: all deaths 41,000; exceeded limit 4,702 (1% needs 8.7%); drugs 8.2%; speeding 3.5%. Hand arithmetic from the published tables reproduces the s5 shares: belt 410/(10155*0.658)=6.1%; speeding 7.7%; exceeded 10.8%; alcohol 4.3%. Drug null share of deaths is 53.9% in 2022-24, so "about half" is correct. The arithmetic is correct.

## 4. Verdict
WEAKENED. Confidence: medium.
The arithmetic in s3-s5 is correct and stable across windows. The weaknesses are interpretive. The s4 text says a camera programme "would have to prevent a far larger share" because "exceeded limit" is smaller. That size depends on unknown and unspecified coding. A different handling of those codes changes the 1% requirement from 8.7% to 4.0-5.9%. The drug segment's apparent difficulty (82% at 10%) is largely a missing-data artefact. The camera recommendation (s6) rests on a crash-conditional severity contrast and ignores the occurrence pathway and the racing component.
Most damaging issue: the s6 sentence "should therefore target limit-exceeding crashes" is a prescription that the severity RR cannot carry, and the s4 comparison of segment sizes ignores the 5,574 deaths/yr with unknown or unspecified exceedance.

## 5. Recommended correction
- Add a sensitivity column to s4 that includes unspecified/unknown speeding deaths, and flag the drugs row as LOW and not comparable.
- Reword s6 from "should therefore target" to "projections should at minimum report limit-exceeding crashes separately; the data do not show that cameras act only on them". Note that racing is included in the segment.
