# P05 red-team report: "Belts act at a different stage"

## 1. Target
reports/alcohol_drugs_restraints.md Summary 5 and §6, README Phase-2 finding 5. The claims: within-vehicle belted/unbelted RR is 0.34 whether or not the driver was speeding ("speeding does not change it"), 0.32 with a BAC>=.08 driver ("only slightly more protective"), and the CRSS unbelted RR falls 46 to 28 to 19 under adjustment. Title of §6: "Stage C, independent of how the crash happened".

## 2. Reproduction
- Committed table `restraint_behaviour/dpc_by_driver_behaviour`: all 0.3415; not speeding 0.3396; speeding 0.3417 (ratio 1.006, p 0.88); BAC>=.08 0.318 vs 0.354 (ratio 0.90, p 0.010).
- My reruns (`01_dpc_strata.py`, 200 bootstrap replicates, same estimator) reproduce these: 0.3415 / 0.3396 / 0.3417, speeding/not ratio 1.006 (0.925–1.095).
- CRSS nested table (46.3, 33.6, 36.0, 28.1, 18.8) reproduced by `03_crss_orderings.py`, which reran the published sample.
- Mechanics: the strata are defined by the driver's vehicle-level behaviour (`own_speeding_type`, `drv_p_p08` > 0.5). Each stratum's driver / right-front-passenger pairs go through the same double-pair estimator.

## 4. Verdict
**WEAKENED**, confidence medium-high.

The headline 0.34 / 0.34 reproduces and the CIs are fine. The inference drawn from it is not robust:
- The speeding "no change" is a marginal coincidence. Speeding is twice as common in single-vehicle and rollover crashes, where belts look more protective, and that offsets a real shift of about +16% (belts less protective with a speeding driver).
- The "slightly more protective with BAC" claim does not survive holding single/multi-vehicle crash fixed (1.03, p 0.46).
- The framing "independent of how the crash happened" is contradicted by the project's own single/multi result and by rollover (0.23 vs 0.43).

Most damaging: attack 1. The published speeding conclusion reverses direction under a basic stratification by the crash type that the report itself shows matters.

## 5. Recommended correction
- Add configuration-stratified (single/multi, rollover) speeding and BAC contrasts to `restraint_behaviour`.
- Reword to: "the pooled belt RR does not differ by speeding; within crash type, belts are somewhat less protective with a speeding driver (about +16%)". Drop "slightly more protective with BAC".
- Retitle §6, since belt RR varies with impact kind.
- Present the CRSS sequence as order-dependent, with the alone-block values.
- Add an equivalence margin where "does not change" is claimed.
