# F02 red-team report: "Adjustment changes the picture"

## 1. Target
reports/findings.md §3 (Finding 2) and README finding 2: night excess "largely accounted for" (crude 3.97 -> 2.46 without light -> 1.62 model A); rain "no less severe than clear on the same surface" (0.71 -> 1.00 [0.89-1.12]); interstates "safer than other roads with the same speed limit" (1.36 -> 0.70), "consistent with divided, access-controlled design"; drivers 65+ "unmasked" (1.13 -> 1.66), "likely mechanism greater physical frailty"; teen drivers "less severe even after adjustment" (0.65 -> 0.86).

## 2. Reproduction
scripts/audit/red_team/F02/01_reproduce_and_dropone.py (full model A, fatal): tod 00-05 = 1.62 (1.46-1.79); rain 1.00 (0.89-1.12); interstate 0.70 (0.60-0.82); 65+ 1.66 (1.51-1.81); 15-20 0.86 (0.78-0.94). All match the published table. Dropping light gives night 2.46 (2.15-2.82), also matching. The estimands are standardised RRs: model A is mutually adjusted, and "Ax" (descendants removed) is the total-association column.

## 4. Verdict
**WEAKENED, medium confidence.** Directions survive (night excess shrinks a lot, rain at fixed surface is ~1, older-driver RR rises, teen RR < 1). Sub-verdicts:
- Night: SURVIVES with caveats (conditional on light and alcohol; total-association retained share is about 65%).
- Rain: WEAKENED. Wording "no less severe" is not supported under KA (0.89, 0.82-0.97) or model B (0.90, 0.81-1.00), and the wet-only estimate is imprecise (1.12, 0.84-1.49). Rain+dry support is essentially empty.
- Interstate: WEAKENED materially. The reversal depends on speed-limit adjustment (0.97 without it) and is absent at 70+ mph (1.00).
- Older: SURVIVES (direction in every speed stratum; magnitude outcome-dependent).
- Teen: SURVIVES for model A; marginal under B.

The single most damaging issue is interstate: the headline "reverse from riskier to safer" is a statement about a speed-limit-matched comparison, with the pooled 0.70 coming from 50-65 mph strata, and the "divided, access-controlled design" explanation is untested.

## 5. Recommended correction
- Interstate: "Interstates are not more severe than other roads once speed limit is held fixed (0.97 without, 0.70 with; no difference at 70+ mph)"; drop or flag as speculation the "divided design" mechanism.
- Rain: "no detectable difference at fixed surface (fatal 1.00, 0.89-1.12); KA outcome shows a modest deficit (0.89, 0.82-0.97); almost all rain crashes are on wet surfaces".
- Night: report the 2.46 (not 1.62) beside any "accounted for" sentence in README/website.
- Add a heterogeneity note for the interstate and older-driver RRs.
