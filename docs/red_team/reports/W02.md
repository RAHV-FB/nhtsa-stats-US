# W02 red-team report: victims of other people's behaviour

## 1. Target
"About 11,800 people a year died in drunk-driving crashes, and 39% of them were not the drunk driver"; "37% of those killed in speeding crashes were not the speeding driver"; large trucks: 85.0% not the truck driver. Sources: road_deaths.md Summary 2, §1, §3, §5, §8; website key findings 1 and 3. Table: outputs/tables/deaths/victim_roles.csv (not_risk_driver rows).

## 2. Reproduction (scripts/audit/red_team/W02/01_reproduce.py, independent of deaths.py, raw interim tables)
- Alcohol: mean over 10 implicates, 105,821 deaths, not-driver share 0.39415. Matches the table (0.394147). 105,821/9 = 11,758 a year, matching "about 11,800".
- Speeding: 98,693 deaths; driver 61,811; not-driver share 0.37370. Matches.
- Large truck: 47,175 deaths; not-driver share 0.85026. Matches.
- MIDRVACC crash membership equals "any driver with P_m >= 8 in MIPER" exactly in implicates 1, 5 and 10 (0 disagreements).
- Role rule: every vehicle whose driver is at .08+ (or coded A_SPVEH, or body 6) is a risk vehicle. Two-drunk-driver crashes therefore do not mislabel a drunk driver as an "other-vehicle occupant". Every speeding crash has at least one A_SPVEH vehicle (02_attacks.py A3: 0 of 98,693 deaths without one).

Attack 9 (measured-BAC-only, 03_measured.py rerun with corrected units; ALC_RES in thousandths per 04_alcres_check.py): restricting to crashes where a driver's MEASURED BAC is >= .08 gives 65,323 deaths (7,258 a year; the published 11,800 a year includes imputed cases). The not-driver share is 27.4% (against 39.4%) and the third-party share is 15.8%. Among non-driver deaths with a test, 2,880 of 5,309 tested passengers had BAC >= .08, and 2,772 of 4,198 tested passengers of the drunk driver's vehicle did (66%). Damage: material. Disclosed: no.

## 4. Verdict: SURVIVES with wording weakening on the "victims" reading. Overall WEAKENED. Confidence: medium-high.
The numbers are exactly reproducible and the role rule is sound. The headline is a correct statement of "not the driver of the drunk vehicle". The most damaging issue is that it invites an "innocent victim" reading. About a third of the non-driver deaths are passengers of the drunk driver, and 3.5% of all deaths are pedestrians who were themselves at .08+. In measured-BAC-only crashes the share falls to 27.4%, and 66% of tested passengers of the drunk driver were themselves at .08+. Separately, the 39% is a mixture: crashes drunk in all implicates have a ~27% non-driver share and imputation-dependent crashes have ~61%.

## 5. Recommended correction
- Add "(including 13% who were the drunk driver's passengers)", or headline "about 27% were people in other vehicles or on foot". For trucks, headline 82% third parties.
- Add one line to §11 and limitations §8 that the share varies with the imputation, with a range of 27.4% (measured-BAC-only crashes) to about 61% (imputation-dependent crashes).
- Note that 3.5% of drunk-crash deaths are pedestrians at .08+.
