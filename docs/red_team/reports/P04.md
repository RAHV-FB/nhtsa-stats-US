## 4. Verdict: WEAKENED (confidence medium)
Core qualitative findings survive: testing follows suspicion and injury; police co-recording exceeds toxicology co-occurrence; alcohol is the stronger correlate of dying than drugs in all tested subsets. Most damaging issue: the headline "1.23 vs 5.0 for the same population" compares different driver sets. On identical drivers it is 1.44 vs 3.51. Secondarily, "1.7 to 73" and "association not identified" rest on extreme differential corners. Plausible allocations give RR about 5-24 and the adjusted RR is about 3-4, so the sign and rough size of the crude and adjusted association are identified. What is not identified is a causal or impairment effect, a different claim.

## 5. Recommended correction
- Report the same-driver ORs (3.5 vs 1.44) or drop "same population".
- Present the bounds as nondifferential 7.8-19 with the 1.7-73 grid labelled as extreme differential corners; say "magnitude not identified; positive in all scenarios".
- Note that the tox "any drug" OR depends on cannabinoids.
- Add the testing-time caveat (not tested here).
