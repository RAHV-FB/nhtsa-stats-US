## Verdict
WEAKENED, confidence medium. I agree with the attacker, with some softening. The headline numbers reproduce exactly and the direction survives (alcohol is larger than speeding; joint is larger than either; no intervention effect is claimed). The undisclosed pooling-window mismatch is a speeding drift inside the CIs. The alcohol coding gap (18.8% vs 12.7%) is real, but the limitations file already discloses the imputation concern. What is overstated is the findings sentence ("shrinks towards zero; the alcohol fraction does not") and the ledger's "robust to plausible differential ascertainment", which the project's own grid contradicts at its corners. The attacker also overstates the inconsistency in (4) and the "lower bound" in (2).
