## Verdict: WEAKENED (medium confidence). I agree with the attacker on direction, but I would downgrade the severity.
The core claims survive: RR 1.25, about half from a smaller denominator (46-54% by outcome), and a within-circumstance RR of about 1.08-1.18 in every variant. What is overstated: "between 28% and 49%" reads as a bound, and the real range across sensible covariate and reference choices is about 25-56%, with intervals wide enough to include 0 for the ref-2020-24 model. The 54% omits its complete-case counterpart. Neither attack is fatal and most of the instability is already disclosed (CI -3 to 77%, drift variables 36%).
