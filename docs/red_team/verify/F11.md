## Judgement: leakage or wording?
- The mechanism is differential ascertainment: the field is coded more thoroughly or more liberally when someone dies, so the outcome influences the input. That is outcome-dependent measurement. In ML terms it is mild target leakage; it is more than a pure wording problem.
- It does not make the model useless: the cost is bounded at about -0.014 to -0.016 AUROC (boosted 0.904, CI 0.894-0.914, still well above the pre-crash-only 0.835), and the HGB-over-logit gap persists.
- The headline sentence is wrong as written, and the "same leakage gate" claim is only a registry-label check. The numerical conclusions survive, but the ablation is not mentioned at the point of the claim.
- Whether the "pre-crash" stage (0.835) contains speeding was not checked by me. If it does, that stage is also not strictly pre-crash.
