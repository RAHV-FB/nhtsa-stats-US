## Verdict: WEAKENED (confidence medium-high). I agree with the attacker.
Overlap is real and large under every scenario (sum >100%, ratio 1.47 to 1.96), so the headline direction, "cannot be added", survives. What is overstated is the wording: "only 65% involve any of them" and "1.68 times" read as measured values although they depend on scoring the 63% of deaths with undetermined drug status as drug-negative. The "at least" is attached to the drug count alone. Attacker's own MAR scenario (83-84%) is speculative and I do not rely on it. This is a wording/caveat problem more than a numerical error; the report body (§4a) is largely candid.

## Recommended corrections (confirmed ones only)
- Summary 1 and README: "sum to at least 109% of all deaths, and at least 65% involve one of them (drug status is undetermined for 63% of deaths and counted as not positive), so each death in this group is counted at least 1.7 times only if ..." Simpler: drop "only"; write "at least 65%"; call the 70% "at least about 70%".
- Give the no-drug figures beside it (three measured factors: sum 85%, union 58%, ratio 1.47) so the reader sees the range.
- Add a line to docs/limitations.md §7 for the union/ratio dependence on undetermined drug status, and a sentence that the unbelted factor applies to occupants only (occupant ratio 1.73 vs 1.14).
- Optionally note the 8% unknown restraint.
