## Verdict
SURVIVES, with wording corrections (confidence: medium-high). I agree with the attacker. The qualitative finding holds under every alternative tried here: exceedance keeps a large RR after alcohol and the other blocks, with and without configuration, and alcohol's share is a minority on every scale. None of the four claims reverses it. The only substantive issue is claim 1: a README headline compares RRs from different adjustment sets and labels neither. It is a wording/materiality issue rather than WEAKENED, because the direction and the "not explained by alcohol" conclusion are unchanged.

## Recommended corrections (confirmed only)
* README finding 2 and Summary B: "Without alcohol the adjusted RR is 6.5 (5.4 when crash configuration is also held fixed), above the alcohol-involved stratum (3.3; 3.7 with configuration); this is the relative scale, and the absolute excess is larger with alcohol (§4)."
* Add to the §4 header: "strata models exclude crash configuration".
* Replace "every recorded circumstance" with "the seven blocks (drugs, distraction and belt use are not in them; adding drugs and distraction gives 4.6, 66% retained)".
* Add that the 9% is the log-RR scale (15% on risk-difference/RR scales; 28% of the part removed by the blocks).
* In §7, qualify "robust to moderate recording bias" with "(shown for the crude RR; the adjusted RR was not re-estimated)". Do not add the 2.8.
